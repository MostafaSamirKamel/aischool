# 11 — STUDENT RESULTS DIRECTORY & PUBLICATION RULES

## 1. Tab 4: Results Organized by Student (النتائج)
- In compliance with the approved specification, Tab 4 organizes all group academic results by student:
  - Student identity (Name, Student Code, Avatar).
  - Homework completion metric: `X / Y واجبات مسلّمة ومصححة`.
  - Average homework score percentage.
  - Exam completion metric: `X / Y امتحانات مكتملة`.
  - Average exam percentage.
  - Academic alert badge: `⚠️ يحتاج متابعة (تحت 50%)` when any homework or exam score falls strictly below 50%.
  - Action button: **"الملف الأكاديمي الشامل"** to open detailed breakdown.

## 2. Detailed Student Academic Profile Modal
When clicking on a student:
1. **Homework Breakdown:** Lists every assignment for the group, session number, submission timestamp, number of attempts, official grade vs max score, instructor feedback, and action to review answer images.
2. **Exam Breakdown:** Lists every exam, attempt count, pass/fail status, official score, and publication status. Unpublished exams show `بانتظار الاعتماد والنشر` to protect unreleased results.

## 3. Preservation of Historical Enrollment Data
- If a student transfers to another group or course cancellation occurs, historical submissions, attempt logs, and official grades are retained.
- The student's academic transcript accurately reflects work submitted while active in the group without resetting progress.
