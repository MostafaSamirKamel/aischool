## 3. Separation of Concerns in Payroll

1. **Accrual vs. Cash Outflow:**
   - Calculating earned instructor fees records an accrued payroll liability.
   - It does **NOT** reduce vault cash until an authorized financial accountant executes the payout.
2. **Pedagogical Ratings Independence:**
   - Teacher evaluation scores generated by educational supervisors serve pedagogical improvement purposes and do **NOT** automatically dock, deduct, or alter contractual salaries.
