# 03 — DESIGN SYSTEM CONSISTENCY AUDIT

**Project:** AI School Management System  
**Audit Dimension:** Cross-Screen Visual Harmony and Component Regularity  

---

## 1. Visual Consistency Pillars Evaluated

1. **Brand Colors Integrity:**
   - Evaluated across buttons, badges, links, active states, and borders.
   - Primary Royal Blue (`#364FAC`) consistently utilized for primary actions, titles, and active indicators.
   - Brand Green (`#58BA47`) consistently applied as the active indicator dot and success badges.
2. **Typography Consistency:**
   - Arabic text across all employee screens renders cleanly with Google Font **`Tajawal`** (Weights 400, 500, 700, 800).
   - Latin text and technical numbers render with **`Inter`** using tabular lining figures.
   - Strict adherence to font scale: H1 (22–24px), Section titles (17–18px), Standard body (14–15px), Tables and Labels (14px).
3. **Spatial & Layout Consistency:**
   - Consistent 8px spacing grid across section margins and field spacings.
   - Standard card border radius: `14px` (`--ai-radius-md`).
   - Standard input and button height: `42–44px`.
   - Standard medium-density table row height: `48–52px`.

---

## 2. Inconsistency Remediation Results
- **Sidebar Background:** Former white background `#FFFFFF` completely superseded by the approved Dark Navy (`#162746`) across all employee panels.
- **Header Uniformity:** Standardized compact 62px header height with integrated breadcrumbs and instant Light/Dark mode toggle.
- **Modals:** Max height constrained to `90vh` with scrollable body to eliminate hidden or clipped action buttons on laptops and mobile devices.

---

## 3. Consistency Verdict
**PASSED.** Complete visual harmony across all administrative, supervision, and instructional pages.
