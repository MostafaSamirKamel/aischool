## 1. Strict Financial Isolation Policy

Student group transfer is strictly an **educational cohort reassignment**. It is **NOT** a commercial sale, cancellation, refund, or re-subscription.

The transfer workflow strictly guarantees that:
1. **No New Enrollment:** The student retains their exact primary `Enrollment` record.
2. **No Enrollment Cancellation:** The current enrollment remains `status = active`.
3. **No Price Recalculation:** `total_amount` remains completely identical before and after transfer.
4. **No Discount Alteration:** Applied discounts (`discount`, discount codes) remain intact.
5. **Installment Schedule Preservation:** Existing installments (`Installment` table), their due dates, paid statuses, and remaining balances are never modified, deleted, or recreated.
6. **No Vault Impact:** School vaults, cash balances, revenue entries, and transaction ledgers undergo zero modifications during a transfer.
7. **Same-Branch Guarantee:** Because transfers are strictly restricted to the same branch (Rule A), financial branch ownership never changes.
