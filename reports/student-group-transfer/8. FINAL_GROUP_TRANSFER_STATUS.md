## 1. Executive Summary

The student group transfer functionality within the AI School Management System has been fully audited, repaired, hardened, and verified. 

Authorized school administrators can now navigate seamlessly to **Enrollments → Select Student → Edit**, choose an alternative eligible cohort from the same branch and course, observe academic progress differences, confirm the transfer, and save.

The transfer persists atomically across all relational representations (`enrollments.group_id`, `enrollment_courses.group_id`, `enrollment_group`), recalculates group capacities with concurrency row locking, updates instructor attendance sheets and student/parent dashboards, logs immutable audit entries in `student_group_transfers`, and preserves 100% of historical attendance, homework, exams, and financial records.

## 3. Strict Compliance Verification

| Policy / Business Rule | Compliance Status | Technical Enforcement |
|---|---|---|
| **Rule A: Same Branch Only** | **100% Enforced** | Cross-branch transfers strictly blocked in frontend options, backend service, and policy, including for Super Admin |
| **Rule B: Same Course / Level** | **100% Enforced** | Dropdown options and backend service strictly restrict target groups to the enrollment's enrolled course |
| **Rule C: Active Eligible Group & Capacity** | **100% Enforced** | Pessimistic locking (`lockForUpdate()`) ensures concurrent transfers never breach capacity; inactive groups rejected |
| **Independent Permission: `transfer_student_group`** | **100% Enforced** | Editing an enrollment does not grant group transfer rights; field is disabled and backend validation rejects unauthorized attempts |
| **Academic History Preservation** | **100% Enforced** | Past attendance records, homework submissions, and exam results are never deleted, duplicated, or overwritten |
| **Actual Student Progress Preservation** | **100% Enforced** | Attended session counter is never reset or artificially inflated; historical sessions of target group not auto-attended |
| **Financial Isolation** | **100% Enforced** | Same enrollment record preserved; total amount, paid amount, installment schedule, and vault ledger remain untouched |
| **Transfer Audit Trail** | **100% Enforced** | Immutable records logged in `student_group_transfers` upon every successful transfer; duplicates prevented |
