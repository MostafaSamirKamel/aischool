**Date:** 2026-10-20  
**Status:** 100% Preservation Verified Across All Subsystems

---

## 1. Core Academic Principles Enforced

A student group transfer is strictly an **administrative adjustment of cohort and schedule**. Under no circumstances does a group transfer:
1. Erase or overwrite historical attendance records.
2. Rewrite past attendance sessions to point to the new group.
3. Automatically mark past sessions of the target group as "attended" by the incoming student.
4. Delete, duplicate, or alter submitted homework assignments and instructor feedback.
5. Invalidate, alter, or duplicate exam scores and evaluations.
6. Revoke access to previously issued learning resources.
7. Reset or artificially inflate the student's actual attended session count.

## 2. Integrity Verification by Academic Subsystem

### 2.1. Attendance Sessions & Historical Records
- **Old Group Sessions:** When Student A attended 4 sessions in Python Group A before transferring to Python Group B:
  - All 4 `AttendanceRecord` rows remain linked to their original `attendance_session_id` and Group A.
  - Check-in timestamps, instructor notes, and status (`present`/`absent`/`late`) are untouched.
- **Target Group Sessions Prior to Transfer:**
  - Python Group B has already held 7 sessions.
  - The transferred student is **NOT** added to those 7 historical sessions.
  - The student's attended count remains exactly 4.
- **Future Sessions:**
  - Beginning with the next scheduled session after transfer, the student appears in Python Group B's attendance sheet.
- **Automated Tests:** **TR-013, TR-020, TR-021** (All PASSED).

### 2.2. Homework Assignments & Submissions
- **Preservation:**
  - Homework completed in the former group remains attached to the original `Assignment` and `AssignmentSubmission` records.
  - Scores, teacher remarks, corrections, and submission dates are preserved.
- **Student Portal Historical Access (`MyAssignments.php`):**
  - The student portal splits assignments into:
    1. **Active Group Assignments:** Live tasks assigned by the new instructor.
    2. **Previous Learning History:** Read-only access to submissions made under previous groups.
- **Automated Tests:** **TR-014** (PASSED).

### 2.3. Exams, Quizzes & Formal Evaluations
- **Integrity Rule:** Exam attempts (`ExamAttempt`), scores, and pass/fail statuses remain strictly tied to the historical assessment context.
- **Instructor Access:** The former instructor retains read-only historical evaluation rights. The new instructor sees the student's historical average without having the authority to alter past grades.
- **Student Portal (`MyExams.php`):** Historical exam attempts and certificates remain visible to the student and parent.
- **Automated Tests:** **TR-015** (PASSED).

### 2.4. Learning Resources & Study Materials
- **Read-Only Historical Access (`StudentLearningResources.php`):**
  - Course slides, PDFs, and recorded videos published to the old group remain accessible to the transferred student under their historical portfolio.
  - The query includes resources where `group_id = current_group_id` OR `group_id IN (historical_transferred_groups)`.
- **New Group Exclusives:**
  - Future exclusive materials published to the old group are not accessible to the student.
  - The student gains immediate access to current and upcoming materials in the new group.
- **Automated Tests:** **TR-016, TR-017** (All PASSED).

### 2.5. Academic Progress Tracking & Difference Warning
- **Course Session Counter:**
  - The student's attended session count is maintained across transfers via `AttendanceRecordObserver.php`.
  - The observer calculates progress per `(student_id, course_id)`, ensuring group migration does not reset or corrupt progress.
- **Progress Difference Warning:**
  - If Target Group Completed Sessions > Student Attended Sessions, a prominent warning alert is displayed in Arabic:
    > "تنبيه: المجموعة الجديدة متقدمة في السيشنز عن تقدم الطالب الحالي. قد يحتاج الطالب إلى مراجعة بعض المحتوى السابق. هل تريد تأكيد النقل؟"
  - The transfer cannot proceed until the user explicitly checks the confirmation checkbox (`confirm_transfer_progress`).
- **Automated Tests:** **TR-018, TR-019** (All PASSED).
